Admissions & Enrollment

Official admission guide for Port Moresby Business College. Review entry requirements for diploma and certificate programs, application pathways, registration policies, and approved school fee schedules.

Application Pathways

How To Apply

Follow the appropriate application procedure based on your current educational status.

DHERST National Online Application System

Grade 12 School Leavers

Grade 12 students completing secondary school in Papua New Guinea must apply through the National Online Application System (NOAS):

  • 1. Contact your school patron or counselor to access your NOAS student account portal.
  • 2. Select Port Moresby Business College as your preferred TVET institution choice.
  • 3. Official selection lists are published by the Department of Education TVET Division following national examinations.
Direct Application Form

Non-School Leavers (NSL)

For self-sponsored candidates, adult entrants, and corporate-sponsored personnel seeking entry:

  • 1. Obtain an official Non-School Leaver Application Form directly from the Boroko campus admissions office.
  • 2. Attach certified copies of Grade 10/12 certificates, academic transcripts, and employer references.
  • 3. Submit completed application forms prior to the advertised annual intake deadline.
Academic Criteria

Entry Requirements

Minimum prerequisite grades required for entry into diploma and certificate courses.

Diploma Programs (2 Years)

  • • Grade 12 Certificate with minimum 'B' or 'C' grades in Language & Literature or Applied English.
  • • Minimum 'B' or 'C' grades in General Mathematics or Major Mathematics.
  • • A 'B' or 'C' grade in a related elective (Business Studies, Accounting, ICT, or Economics).
  • • For Non-School Leavers: Grade 10 with 'Credit' grades plus at least 2 years of relevant work experience.

Technical Certificate Courses (TTC)

  • • Grade 12 Certificate with minimum 'C' grades in English and Mathematics.
  • • Grade 10 Certificate with 'Credit' passes in Mathematics and English (no Fail grades).
  • • Verified academic records free of altered or unauthenticated marks.
  • • Demonstrated competence in practical business computing and office management fundamentals.

Enrollment Policy

Registration & Certificate Rules

During orientation week, accepted students must complete formal physical registration at the Boroko campus.

Original Certificates Required

Students must physically present original Grade 10 and Grade 12 certificates. Photocopied documents are not accepted.

Defaced Certificates Policy

Laminated, defaced, altered, or damaged original certificates will be rejected by the Registrar's Office during registration.

Fraud Prevention

Presenting fraudulent credentials will result in immediate expulsion and referral to the Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary.
