Our Heritage

Institutional History

Port Moresby Business College is one of the oldest institutions that has been contributing directly to the development of Papua New Guinea's human resources.

The institution was not known by its current name from its inception. In the early 1970s (1972–1974), it was established and operated strictly as a Secretarial College. It continued functioning in this capacity until December 1986, delivering practical administrative and office skills to serve government and commercial offices.

In 1987, following the Department of Education’s implementation of its rationalization policy on national education, the institution was officially accorded Business College status. This transition brought significant changes to the academic curriculum, transformed training pathways, and led to a steady rise in student intake from across all provinces in Papua New Guinea.

Over the decades, as student enrollment expanded, the College Governing Council initiated donor-funded infrastructure projects to construct additional facilities and residential housing to support its growing team of national and expatriate academic staff.

National Impact Today:

Today, Port Moresby Business College hosts over 600 students annually from all 22 provinces, maintaining a tradition of innovative teaching tailored to the needs of modern industry and society.

How We Teach

Pedagogical Philosophy

Our practical educational model ensures students master real-world tools required by PNG employers.

Methodology 01

Practical Learning Delivery

Courses are delivered through lectures, tutorials, practical workshops, business case studies, computer simulations, and real-time research projects.

Methodology 02

High Quality Service Delivery

Maintaining the highest standards across academic programs, student support services, and instructional materials provided by skilled national and expatriate faculty.

Methodology 03

Affordable Tuition Policy

Maintaining affordable tuition fees consonant with high service standards, ensuring equitable access for students across Papua New Guinea.

Methodology 04

Action-Oriented Experience

Engaging students in immersive out-of-class practical activities, community projects, and research tasks that bridge theory with workplace practice.

Methodology 05

Human Capacity Building

Building national institutional capacity for sustainable human resource development aligned with PNG's Medium Term Development Strategy (MTDS).

Methodology 06

Academic Partnerships

Pursuing growth through partial autonomy in program delivery alongside affiliations with established tertiary institutions and university networks.
