Technical Training Certificates (TTC)

Intensive 1-year certificate programs designed for immediate placement in operational, technical, and administrative roles.

TTC Level

Hands-on vocational instruction covering front desk operations, food preparation, dining room service, basic housekeeping, and local tour guiding principles.

Duration: 1 Year

Certificate in Business Studies

Covers entry-level commercial skills, introductory bookkeeping, keyboarding, business mathematics, and workplace communication.

Duration: 1 Year
TTC Level 2

Focuses on ledger posting, cash handling, payroll processing, bank reconciliation, and basic computer accounting software.

Duration: 1 Year

Provides practical instruction in document processing, filing systems, customer service, front office reception, and clerical support.

Duration: 1 Year
Academic Progression

UPNG Program Accreditation MOU

Port Moresby Business College maintains a formal Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) for academic program accreditation with the University of Papua New Guinea (UPNG).

Eligible POMBC graduates who complete their 2-year National Diploma with high academic standings can apply for credit transfers to articulate directly into Year 3 of relevant Bachelor Degree programs at the UPNG School of Business and Public Policy.

Degree Advancement
Diploma ➔ Degree

Direct pathway into 3rd Year Bachelor Degree studies at UPNG for qualifying graduates.
